# Report chart styles: configure SMN with confidence

Chart styles create consistent visuals in generated Office reports.

> **Camille's tip:** check what already exists, then modify only what is necessary.

## 1. Why is this setting important?

Chart styles create consistent visuals in generated Office reports. Good configuration makes subsequent data easier to understand and reduces repeated manual work.

## 2. Open the configuration screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Report chart styles** in the corresponding section.
3. Use the available entry to open [the settings screen](/app/admin/settings/reports/charts).
4. Check permissions and current records before making changes.

## 3. A step-by-step approach

1. Review the gallery before modifying a style.
2. Select a style matching the intended chart type.
3. Use the gallery preview when available.
4. Customize and save only if authorized.
5. Generate a Word or PowerPoint report to verify the outcome.

## 4. Understand the fields and their impact

Styles define presentation; numbers still come from indicators and data requested by template tags.

> **What happens when you save?** The relevant configuration becomes available to its business module. Previous records or generated documents may not be changed retrospectively.

## 5. Advice and troubleshooting

A gauge, line and bar chart tell different stories. Choose the form based on the measure, not just appearance.

If the action is missing, check your role and whether the catalog marks it as unavailable. Review any validation error before repeating the change.

## Your final check

Can you explain what changed, why it matters, and where to verify the result? If yes, the setup is ready for use.
