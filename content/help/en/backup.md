# Verify SMN backups

A useful backup is one you can find and verify. Here's how to check your archives without accidentally restoring data.

## Before you begin

Access to backup features requires the appropriate account permissions.

## Follow the steps

- Open [Settings > Maintenance](/app/admin/settings/maintenance).
- Open the backup tab when it is available to your account.
- Review the archive inventory, statuses and verification results.
- If authorized, start a backup from the business interface and verify its outcome.
- Manually mark **backup** done once your procedure has been checked.

## Restore safeguards

An available or verified backup does not mean restoration is authorized. Follow SMN's displayed security checks. Do not replace the user interface with manual commands.

Help checklist progress is local to the browser and is not included in backup archives.
