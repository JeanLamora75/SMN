# Capacity settings: configure SMN with confidence

Settings lists Capacity as planned, but no dedicated configuration screen is currently linked. Available capacity calculations rely on member data.

> **Before you change anything:** look at existing settings and consider which teams and users they affect.

## 1. Why configure this section?

Settings lists Capacity as planned, but no dedicated configuration screen is currently linked. Available capacity calculations rely on member data. Review it whenever your organization changes, or when a feature's results do not match your expectations.

## 2. Open the right screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Capacity settings** in the settings catalog.
3. Select its entry, when available. The related screen is [here](/app/admin/settings).
4. Check your access rights, the selected team and the current values.

## 3. Follow the configuration workflow

1. Find Capacity in Settings and check its availability status.
2. To work with capacity today, open Members.
3. Review assignments, working patterns, absences and member countries.
4. Review the available capacity indicators for cycles.

## 4. Understand the fields and their effects

Person-day capacity depends on assignment dates, roles, worked half-days and absences. A listed entry does not imply the settings form is implemented.

> **What changes after Save?** The resulting configuration is used by the related SMN module. Existing business records or already generated documents are not necessarily changed retroactively.

## 5. Practical guidance

Do not attempt to configure an unavailable feature; complete member data and check calculated results instead.

**If the option is unavailable**, check your permissions, the installation mode and whether the Settings catalog marks it as planned or only partially available.

## Your final check

Can you explain **what was changed, why, what it affects and how to verify it in the relevant screen**? If yes, the setup is meaningful and ready for daily use.
