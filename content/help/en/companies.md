# Companies: configure SMN with confidence

Companies provide organizational reference records. Clear records help avoid duplicates and make it easier to select the correct organization.

> **Before you change anything:** look at existing settings and consider which teams and users they affect.

## 1. Why configure this section?

Companies provide organizational reference records. Clear records help avoid duplicates and make it easier to select the correct organization. Review it whenever your organization changes, or when a feature's results do not match your expectations.

## 2. Open the right screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Companies** in the settings catalog.
3. Select its entry, when available. The related screen is [here](/app/admin/companies).
4. Check your access rights, the selected team and the current values.

## 3. Follow the configuration workflow

1. Open Companies and review existing records before creating anything.
2. Search for an existing company to avoid duplicates.
3. Use the creation action and complete the fields shown in the form.
4. Save and verify the new entry; use Edit for corrections.

## 4. Understand the fields and their effects

The company name is the recognizable label in lists. Check the other fields shown before saving.

> **What changes after Save?** The resulting configuration is used by the related SMN module. Existing business records or already generated documents are not necessarily changed retroactively.

## 5. Practical guidance

Do not delete a company merely because its name has changed; update the existing record first.

**If the option is unavailable**, check your permissions, the installation mode and whether the Settings catalog marks it as planned or only partially available.

## Your final check

Can you explain **what was changed, why, what it affects and how to verify it in the relevant screen**? If yes, the setup is meaningful and ready for daily use.
