# Review general settings

## Prerequisites

You need permission to access settings. Editing them requires appropriate privileges, normally administrator rights.

## Steps

- Open [General settings](/app/admin/settings/general).
- Review the fields and options actually shown by the form.
- Change the required options and use the screen's save action.
- Check the feedback and, where useful, reload the page to verify the values.
- Manually mark the **general** step done in the Help center.

## Result

The business screen saves settings, not the Help center. Users without the necessary permissions must not attempt configuration changes.
