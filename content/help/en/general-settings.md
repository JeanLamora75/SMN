# General settings: language, appearance and calendar reference

## Access and prerequisites

Open [General settings](/app/admin/settings/general) using an authorized administrator account. Settings are saved by this business form, not by the Help center.

## Understand the three tabs

- **General**: view the read-only **Agile mode** and **product**. Select a **reference country** from the list when needed.
- **Language**: choose **French** or **English** from the currently available languages.
- **Appearance**: choose the **light or dark theme** and an offered **palette** (Ocean, Forest, Violet or Red/Purple).

Agile mode and product are fixed information in this form; they cannot be edited here.

## Change and save settings

1. Open the tab for the setting you want to change.
2. Choose the desired value from its selector. For the reference country, you can leave the selection empty when offered.
3. An alert indicates **unsaved changes**. Select **Save**; the button is disabled when nothing changed or while saving.
4. Read the confirmation or error message. Display settings such as language, theme or palette may refresh the interface after saving.
5. If needed, use **Reload** to restore the currently saved values. Check the revision or update date displayed by the form.

## Errors and precautions

- **Revision conflict**: settings changed since the form loaded. Reload and review the latest values before trying again.
- **Invalid value**: choose an allowed option and retry.
- **Load or network error**: use the retry action and check the local connection to SMN.
- **Pending edits**: save or discard your changes before leaving; SMN warns about unsaved drafts.
- **Access denied**: a user without administrative rights must not attempt to change settings.

## Final check

Confirm the intended values are saved and displayed. The Help center checklist does not validate settings automatically: mark **general** done only after your own review.
