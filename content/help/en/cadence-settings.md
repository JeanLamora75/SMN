# Team cadence: configure SMN with confidence

Cadence defines cycle timing and drives suggested dates when generating Scrum sprints or Kanban periods.

> **Before you change anything:** look at existing settings and consider which teams and users they affect.

## 1. Why configure this section?

Cadence defines cycle timing and drives suggested dates when generating Scrum sprints or Kanban periods. Review it whenever your organization changes, or when a feature's results do not match your expectations.

## 2. Open the right screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Team cadence** in the settings catalog.
3. Select its entry, when available. The related screen is [here](/app/admin/scrum-cadence).
4. Check your access rights, the selected team and the current values.

## 3. Follow the configuration workflow

1. From Settings, open Team cadence for the relevant Agile mode.
2. Choose the team and review its displayed configuration.
3. For Scrum, check duration, start/end weekdays and times, and the time zone.
4. For Kanban, check period settings and any cadence proposal.
5. Save, then preview a cycle before generating it.

## 4. Understand the fields and their effects

Sprint dates are calculated from the team's saved cadence. If no Scrum cadence exists, SMN can initialize defaults: two weeks, Monday 09:00 to Friday 18:00, Europe/Paris.

> **What changes after Save?** The resulting configuration is used by the related SMN module. Existing business records or already generated documents are not necessarily changed retroactively.

## 5. Practical guidance

Check cadence before generating six sprints: changing settings later does not automatically replace every existing period.

**If the option is unavailable**, check your permissions, the installation mode and whether the Settings catalog marks it as planned or only partially available.

## Your final check

Can you explain **what was changed, why, what it affects and how to verify it in the relevant screen**? If yes, the setup is meaningful and ready for daily use.
