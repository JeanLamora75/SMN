# Prepare the team calendar

## Prerequisites

A team must exist. Editing requires the appropriate privileges.

## Steps

- Open the [Team calendar](/app/team-calendar) and select your team context.
- Review displayed days, cycle periods, absences and available calendar data.
- Check individual calendars and assignments under [Team / Members](/app/admin/members).
- If the installation includes a Scrum team, an administrator may access [working-day settings](/app/admin/settings/working-days). This screen is not meant for Kanban-only installations.
- Check results after making changes in the business screens, then manually mark **calendar** done.

## Note

Absences, public holidays and availability can differ between team members. Viewing a calendar does not automatically verify capacity.
