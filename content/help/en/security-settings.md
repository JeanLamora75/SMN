# Security and access: configure SMN with confidence

Security covers password changes and recovery information managed by administrators.

> **Camille's tip:** check what already exists, then modify only what is necessary.

## 1. Why is this setting important?

Security covers password changes and recovery information managed by administrators. Good configuration makes subsequent data easier to understand and reduces repeated manual work.

## 2. Open the configuration screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Security and access** in the corresponding section.
3. Use the available entry to open [the settings screen](/app/security).
4. Check permissions and current records before making changes.

## 3. A step-by-step approach

1. Open Security and find the password-change section.
2. Complete the fields according to the on-screen requirements.
3. Submit and read the confirmation.
4. Administrators can review the recovery section when present.
5. Keep sensitive information confidential.

## 4. Understand the fields and their impact

A password protects account access. Recovery and session invalidation can have separate consequences.

> **What happens when you save?** The relevant configuration becomes available to its business module. Previous records or generated documents may not be changed retrospectively.

## 5. Advice and troubleshooting

Never place passwords or recovery phrases in tickets, reports or shared notes.

If the action is missing, check your role and whether the catalog marks it as unavailable. Review any validation error before repeating the change.

## Your final check

Can you explain what changed, why it matters, and where to verify the result? If yes, the setup is ready for use.
