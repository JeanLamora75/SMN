# Registers: risks, impediments, dependencies, decisions, actions and highlights

An early risk or a well-recorded decision can prevent confusion later. Use registries to make important team information visible and actionable.

## Access registers

Open [Registers](/app/admin/registries) after selecting a team your account can access. The data shown depends on the team and, when offered, the selected cycle.

The main list distinguishes **risks**, **impediments** and **dependencies**. SMN also supports **decisions**, **actions** and **highlights**, including in related record details and journals. Do not expect six separate tabs on the main list.

## Browse and find an entry

- On the list, choose an available category or use the search and filters provided (status, cycle, sorting and other criteria visible according to your permissions).
- Open a record to review its title, date, status, description and, where available, type-specific details, relationships and journal.
- Check the team and cycle before comparing information. The list can be empty even when records exist in another context.
- Users without management permission can view entries in read-only mode; editing and deletion actions are not shown to them.

## Understand the six types

### Risk

A **risk** describes something that might happen. Its statuses are **Open** and **Resolved**. You can also provide a **probability**, an **impact** and a **ROAM** response during creation. These assessments are separate from the risk's status.

### Impediment

An **impediment** describes a difficulty that has occurred. Its possible statuses are **Open**, **In progress**, **Resolved** and **Abandoned**. Review the status changes and the details recorded in its entry.

### Dependency

A **dependency** describes coordination between a **requester** and a **contributor**, each associated with the local team or an external party. It can also be marked **at risk**. Its statuses are **Open**, **In progress**, **Satisfied** and **Cancelled**.

If you enter the parties in the form, the requester and contributor must be specified consistently; an external party requires a name.

### Decision

A **decision** records a choice and, where provided, its **rationale**. It can reference a previous decision that it **replaces**. The statuses are **Active**, **Replaced** and **Cancelled**.

Decisions can also be found through related record journals or relationships. Follow the creation context instead of expecting them among the main category tabs.

### Action

An **action** represents work or follow-up to perform. Its statuses are **To do**, **In progress**, **Completed** and **Cancelled**. Depending on your permissions, its record may display links to other items and a tracking history.

### Highlight

A **highlight** records a notable event or piece of information. It uses the **Recorded** status. It may be created or found through a related journal, depending on the actions provided by that record; it does not create a new main-list tab.

## Create, edit or delete

- If your account offers the **Create** action, open the creation dialog and select a type available in that context. The form asks for a **title**, **date** and **status**, then shows additional fields for the chosen type.
- Available types may be restricted when creation starts from a journal. Use the displayed options rather than trying to force an unavailable type.
- With appropriate permissions, open a record and use the available **edit** actions.
- **Permanent deletion** is separate from reading a record. It requires management permission and can be refused when linked data, a conflict or a protection prevents the operation. Review the record and consequences before confirming.
- Reading this article does not create, update or delete any entry.

## Common issues

- **No entries**: check the team, cycle, category and filters before assuming information is missing.
- **Action not shown**: your role or the creation context may restrict available actions and types.
- **Form rejected**: verify the title, required fields and, for dependencies, the requester and contributor.
- **Deletion rejected**: review the record's message and linked items. Do not bypass a protective refusal.
