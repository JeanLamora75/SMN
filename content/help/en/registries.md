# Registers: risks, impediments and dependencies

## Access

Open [Registers](/app/admin/registries) after selecting an authorized team. The information displayed is scoped to your account permissions and team context.

## Browsing

- Review entries and select a register type if filtering is available.
- Open an entry to inspect its information, state and related items.
- Check team and cycle context before interpreting its details.

## Management

- If authorized, use the creation and editing actions provided by the business screen.
- Review business impact before any permanent deletion; Help never deletes entries.
- A missing action may reflect your permissions or installation settings.

## Common issues

If the screen reports that the team is unavailable, check the active team and your access. An empty list does not imply that an entry should be created.
