# Plan and generate your Scrum sprints

Preparing your next sprint or a whole series? SMN helps you build a consistent sprint calendar without entering every date by hand. The key is to **choose the right starting point, review the proposal and confirm only when everything looks correct**.

> **Camille's tip:** check existing sprints before creating any. Your installation may already contain sample sprints you can explore.

## 1. Find your team's sprints

1. Open [Sprints](/app/admin/sprints) from the navigation.
2. Check the team shown in the header: this is the context for the sprint list.
3. Explore the **All**, **Active**, **Planned**, **Finished** and **Closed** status filters.
4. Use **search** and **sort** when the list gets longer. Select a sprint name to open its details.

**Why start here?** Creating another series without checking the current schedule can lead to overlaps or unnecessary sprints. If the sprint already exists, open it instead of generating a duplicate.

## 2. Open the generation wizard

With administrator permissions, select **Generate** from the list header. The wizard has three stages: **Configure**, **Preview** and **Confirm**.

### What each field means

**Number of sprints** — Choose how many sprints to create together, from **1 to 6**. Start with **1** while exploring; select more when planning several iterations ahead.

**Start date** — Enter the start date of the first sprint. SMN may suggest a date, but you should check it before continuing. The other dates are calculated using the configured cadence.

**Cadence** — The configured duration, weekdays, times and time zone determine the proposed periods. These settings are not all entered in this wizard. If the calculated dates seem wrong, review the cadence settings before confirming.

> **Example:** to plan three consecutive sprints, enter **3** for the number of sprints and choose the first start date. SMN proposes three periods for review. Their actual dates depend on your team's cadence.

Select **Show preview** to continue. You are still reviewing a proposal; no sprint has been confirmed yet.

## 3. Review the preview carefully

The preview is your opportunity to catch mistakes before they become part of your schedule.

**Sprint name** — SMN proposes a name that you can edit, up to **160 characters**. Use a consistent naming convention so teammates can find each sprint easily.

**Start and end dates** — Check the calculated periods and the displayed **time zone**. Pay particular attention to the first and last dates and the transition between periods.

**Conflicts** — This column identifies overlaps with existing or proposed sprints. If a conflict appears, return to configuration and adjust the proposal.

**Ceremony templates** — Active templates may be listed so you know which ceremony templates are available for generation.

**Historical ceremonies** — When working with past dates, an additional choice may appear. Read the warning carefully: it concerns historical ceremony handling, not sprint duration.

### Changed a name or an option?

Select **Refresh preview**. The wizard requires an up-to-date proposal before you can proceed.

## 4. Confirm the generation

1. Check the **number**, **names**, **dates** and any blocking conflicts.
2. Select **Continue** to reach the confirmation stage.
3. Review the sprint names and any warning displayed.
4. Select **Create** only when you are satisfied.

SMN returns to the sprint list. Find the new sprints and open one to inspect its saved details.

## 5. Troubleshooting

- **Generate is missing:** creating sprints requires administrator permissions.
- **The dates look wrong:** review the starting date and configured cadence before confirming.
- **A conflict is shown:** check sprints already scheduled for those dates and update your proposal.
- **You edited a sprint name:** select **Refresh preview** before continuing.
- **The Sprints page is unavailable:** check that the installation uses Scrum mode and you have the necessary access. Kanban periods follow a different process.

## Your final check

You should be able to answer: **which sprints exist, what dates do they cover, and are they in the right team context?** Once you can answer those questions, your sprint calendar is ready.

> **Remember:** this guide does not create sprints. Creation happens only in SMN's wizard after you confirm the operation.
