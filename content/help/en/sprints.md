# Prepare your first Scrum sprint

## Prerequisites

You need access to a Scrum team. Creation operations require appropriate privileges. This guide does not apply to Kanban.

## Steps

- Open [Sprints](/app/admin/sprints).
- Select the relevant Scrum team.
- Review existing sprints, or use the generation action if you are an administrator.
- Check cadence, dates and statuses before confirming a business operation.
- Verify the sprint in the list afterwards and manually mark **cadence** done.

## Note

SMN may redirect to the home page when Scrum mode or permissions are unavailable. The Help center does not generate sprints.
