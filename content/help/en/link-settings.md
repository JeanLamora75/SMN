# Link settings: configure SMN with confidence

These settings manage link inventory and categories. Links can then be browsed in Links and reused by compatible dashboards or reports.

> **Before you change anything:** look at existing settings and consider which teams and users they affect.

## 1. Why configure this section?

These settings manage link inventory and categories. Links can then be browsed in Links and reused by compatible dashboards or reports. Review it whenever your organization changes, or when a feature's results do not match your expectations.

## 2. Open the right screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Link settings** in the settings catalog.
3. Select its entry, when available. The related screen is [here](/app/admin/settings/links).
4. Check your access rights, the selected team and the current values.

## 3. Follow the configuration workflow

1. Open Settings then Links.
2. Under Inventory, review entries and detect outdated resources.
3. Under Categories, create or edit categories offered when adding a link.
4. Check names, URLs and Team or Cross-team scope.
5. Save, then open Links to verify filters and visibility.

## 4. Understand the fields and their effects

Category organizes resources; scope distinguishes team-specific and cross-team references.

> **What changes after Save?** The resulting configuration is used by the related SMN module. Existing business records or already generated documents are not necessarily changed retroactively.

## 5. Practical guidance

Use recognizable categories. Avoid creating dozens of nearly identical ones.

**If the option is unavailable**, check your permissions, the installation mode and whether the Settings catalog marks it as planned or only partially available.

## Your final check

Can you explain **what was changed, why, what it affects and how to verify it in the relevant screen**? If yes, the setup is meaningful and ready for daily use.
