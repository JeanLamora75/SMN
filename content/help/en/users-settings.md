# User accounts: configure SMN with confidence

Accounts determine who can log in to SMN and which operations they may perform; they are not the same as Member profiles.

> **Camille's tip:** check what already exists, then modify only what is necessary.

## 1. Why is this setting important?

Accounts determine who can log in to SMN and which operations they may perform; they are not the same as Member profiles. Good configuration makes subsequent data easier to understand and reduces repeated manual work.

## 2. Open the configuration screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **User accounts** in the corresponding section.
3. Use the available entry to open [the settings screen](/app/admin/users).
4. Check permissions and current records before making changes.

## 3. A step-by-step approach

1. Search the account list first.
2. Select Create account if available.
3. Enter the required details and choose an appropriate role.
4. Save and check the status and permissions.
5. Before disabling an account, review its use and organizational access needs.

## 4. Understand the fields and their impact

An account controls application permissions; a Member profile tracks a person's assignment and availability.

> **What happens when you save?** The relevant configuration becomes available to its business module. Previous records or generated documents may not be changed retrospectively.

## 5. Advice and troubleshooting

Grant the minimum access needed. Keep account administration separate from team member management.

If the action is missing, check your role and whether the catalog marks it as unavailable. Review any validation error before repeating the change.

## Your final check

Can you explain what changed, why it matters, and where to verify the result? If yes, the setup is ready for use.
