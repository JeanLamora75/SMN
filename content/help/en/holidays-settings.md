# Countries and holidays: configure SMN with confidence

National holidays are associated with each member's location country. This section lets you review the country references used by calendars.

> **Before you change anything:** look at existing settings and consider which teams and users they affect.

## 1. Why configure this section?

National holidays are associated with each member's location country. This section lets you review the country references used by calendars. Review it whenever your organization changes, or when a feature's results do not match your expectations.

## 2. Open the right screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Countries and holidays** in the settings catalog.
3. Select its entry, when available. The related screen is [here](/app/admin/settings/holidays-closures#holidays).
4. Check your access rights, the selected team and the current values.

## 3. Follow the configuration workflow

1. Open Settings then Holidays.
2. Review Countries / public holidays.
3. Check available country entries and the information displayed.
4. In a member profile, verify the two-letter ISO country code.
5. Return to the member calendar and check the expected holidays.

## 4. Understand the fields and their effects

SMN looks up holidays for each member's country; local holidays may not be covered.

> **What changes after Save?** The resulting configuration is used by the related SMN module. Existing business records or already generated documents are not necessarily changed retroactively.

## 5. Practical guidance

If a holiday seems missing, first check the member's location and country reference before adding an absence.

**If the option is unavailable**, check your permissions, the installation mode and whether the Settings catalog marks it as planned or only partially available.

## Your final check

Can you explain **what was changed, why, what it affects and how to verify it in the relevant screen**? If yes, the setup is meaningful and ready for daily use.
