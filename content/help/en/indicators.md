# Dynamic indicators

## Prerequisites

You must be signed in and have access to at least one team. Editing the catalog and permissions depends on your account role.

## View indicators

- Open [Indicators](/app/admin/indicators).
- Select an authorized team and review its Scrum or Kanban indicators.
- Check cycle values and the available displays in the relevant screens.
- Indicators may use manual values or formulas; a missing value is not necessarily zero.

## Administration

- An administrator can review indicator settings from Settings, including definitions and permissions.
- Before changing a formula, inspect its dependencies and potential effects on dashboards and reports.
- If an action is unavailable, review your role and selected team rather than bypassing access checks.

## Common issues

- **No indicator or value**: review team/cycle context, permissions and the underlying data needed for calculation.
- **Unexpected result**: check the formula and dependencies before treating it as a defect.
- **Access denied**: ask an administrator to review your permissions.

The Help center never edits indicators or formulas.
