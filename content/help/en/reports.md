# Explore reports

## Prerequisites

You need a signed-in account, an accessible team and suitable reporting data.

## Steps

- Open [Reports](/app/reports).
- Review the available tabs and templates according to your permissions.
- Select the team and cycle if prompted by the page.
- View existing documents; if authorized, use the available creation features.
- Check the outcome before manually marking **reports** done.

## Important

An available template does not guarantee that the required data is present. Help never generates documents on your behalf.
