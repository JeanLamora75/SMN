# Company closures: configure SMN with confidence

A company closure is a planned collective closure, separate from individual absences and national public holidays.

> **Before you change anything:** look at existing settings and consider which teams and users they affect.

## 1. Why configure this section?

A company closure is a planned collective closure, separate from individual absences and national public holidays. Review it whenever your organization changes, or when a feature's results do not match your expectations.

## 2. Open the right screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Company closures** in the settings catalog.
3. Select its entry, when available. The related screen is [here](/app/admin/settings/holidays-closures#closures).
4. Check your access rights, the selected team and the current values.

## 3. Follow the configuration workflow

1. Open Settings then Company closures.
2. Choose the Closures tab on the holidays and closures page.
3. Review existing periods to avoid duplicates.
4. Create or edit using the fields offered by the form, if authorized.
5. Save and check the relevant calendar dates.

## 4. Understand the fields and their effects

A collective closure affects planning and should not be confused with individual absence or country-specific public holidays.

> **What changes after Save?** The resulting configuration is used by the related SMN module. Existing business records or already generated documents are not necessarily changed retroactively.

## 5. Practical guidance

Before creating a closure, check that it truly applies at company level rather than only to an individual or team.

**If the option is unavailable**, check your permissions, the installation mode and whether the Settings catalog marks it as planned or only partially available.

## Your final check

Can you explain **what was changed, why, what it affects and how to verify it in the relevant screen**? If yes, the setup is meaningful and ready for daily use.
