# Register settings: configure SMN with confidence

Register settings help manage automatic highlights and deadline alerts. They complement risk, impediment and dependency records.

> **Before you change anything:** look at existing settings and consider which teams and users they affect.

## 1. Why configure this section?

Register settings help manage automatic highlights and deadline alerts. They complement risk, impediment and dependency records. Review it whenever your organization changes, or when a feature's results do not match your expectations.

## 2. Open the right screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Register settings** in the settings catalog.
3. Select its entry, when available. The related screen is [here](/app/admin/settings/registries).
4. Check your access rights, the selected team and the current values.

## 3. Follow the configuration workflow

1. Open Settings then Registers.
2. Under Categories, review automatic highlight settings.
3. Under Alerts, review deadline notification rules.
4. Change only necessary options, according to your permissions.
5. Save and check affected alerts and register records.

## 4. Understand the fields and their effects

Alerts highlight dates or situations to review; they do not resolve a risk or impediment automatically.

> **What changes after Save?** The resulting configuration is used by the related SMN module. Existing business records or already generated documents are not necessarily changed retroactively.

## 5. Practical guidance

Assign someone to review alerts regularly; a rule without follow-up becomes noise.

**If the option is unavailable**, check your permissions, the installation mode and whether the Settings catalog marks it as planned or only partially available.

## Your final check

Can you explain **what was changed, why, what it affects and how to verify it in the relevant screen**? If yes, the setup is meaningful and ready for daily use.
