# Indicator catalog: configure SMN with confidence

The catalog defines measurements used by SMN. Manual indicators receive cycle values; computed indicators use formulas over available data.

> **Before you change anything:** look at existing settings and consider which teams and users they affect.

## 1. Why configure this section?

The catalog defines measurements used by SMN. Manual indicators receive cycle values; computed indicators use formulas over available data. Review it whenever your organization changes, or when a feature's results do not match your expectations.

## 2. Open the right screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Indicator catalog** in the settings catalog.
3. Select its entry, when available. The related screen is [here](/app/admin/settings/indicators).
4. Check your access rights, the selected team and the current values.

## 3. Follow the configuration workflow

1. Open Settings then Indicators and review the Catalog.
2. Select Create and define name, unique tag, type, description and unit.
3. For a computed indicator, enter a formula supported by the Expression guide.
4. Save, and preview impacts before changing an existing formula.
5. In Indicators, select relevant measures for each team and configure displays.

## 4. Understand the fields and their effects

The tag identifies the indicator in formulas and Office templates; the description states the business meaning of the measure.

> **What changes after Save?** The resulting configuration is used by the related SMN module. Existing business records or already generated documents are not necessarily changed retroactively.

## 5. Practical guidance

Avoid renaming tags already present in Word or PowerPoint templates: SMN does not rewrite existing Office files.

**If the option is unavailable**, check your permissions, the installation mode and whether the Settings catalog marks it as planned or only partially available.

## Your final check

Can you explain **what was changed, why, what it affects and how to verify it in the relevant screen**? If yes, the setup is meaningful and ready for daily use.
