# Jira settings

A useful Jira integration starts with clear settings. Follow the steps in order and verify the configuration before relying on exchanged data.

## Before you begin

Jira configuration is restricted to administrators. Accessing the page does not guarantee that your environment can reach an external Jira service.

## Follow the steps

- Open [Jira settings](/app/admin/settings/jira).
- Review the fields and current values presented by the form.
- Enter only the necessary configuration details and follow the available verification feedback.
- Save through the business form and check the result before considering the integration configured.

## Security

Never include tokens, passwords or other private details in Help articles or public screenshots. Non-administrative accounts must not be directed to this restricted settings page.
