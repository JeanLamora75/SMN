# Working days and hours: configure SMN with confidence

This configuration defines work slots and lunch rules for Scrum. It complements each member's personal work calendar.

> **Before you change anything:** look at existing settings and consider which teams and users they affect.

## 1. Why configure this section?

This configuration defines work slots and lunch rules for Scrum. It complements each member's personal work calendar. Review it whenever your organization changes, or when a feature's results do not match your expectations.

## 2. Open the right screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Working days and hours** in the settings catalog.
3. Select its entry, when available. The related screen is [here](/app/admin/settings/working-days).
4. Check your access rights, the selected team and the current values.

## 3. Follow the configuration workflow

1. Open Settings then Working days in a Scrum context.
2. Under Slots, verify the team and proposed work windows.
3. Adjust working days and hours to match team practice.
4. Under Lunch break, review the configured lunch rule.
5. Save and check the calendar and individual schedules.

## 4. Understand the fields and their effects

Team slots provide a shared frame; an individual's schedule may differ and should be recorded in their member profile.

> **What changes after Save?** The resulting configuration is used by the related SMN module. Existing business records or already generated documents are not necessarily changed retroactively.

## 5. Practical guidance

Do not model a one-off absence as a permanent working-day change. Use absences for exceptions.

**If the option is unavailable**, check your permissions, the installation mode and whether the Settings catalog marks it as planned or only partially available.

## Your final check

Can you explain **what was changed, why, what it affects and how to verify it in the relevant screen**? If yes, the setup is meaningful and ready for daily use.
