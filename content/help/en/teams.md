# Create a team

## Prerequisites

An authorized administrator should first review general settings.

## Steps

- Open [Settings > Teams](/app/admin/settings/teams).
- Use the create-team button in the page header.
- Complete the fields shown in the form, including the name and Agile mode when offered.
- Save, then verify that the team appears in the list.
- Return to the checklist and declare **team** done when the team actually exists.

## Scrum or Kanban

The team's mode controls which screens and features apply. Sprints belong to Scrum; Kanban periods belong to Kanban. Help never creates a team on your behalf.
