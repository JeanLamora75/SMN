# Review and customize your team

## Why this matters

SMN associates members, cycles and business information with a team. **Check for an existing team before creating another one.** An existing or restored installation may contain teams to customize. A fresh native v33 installation on an empty database, however, does not create a team automatically.

Editing and creating teams require administrator permissions.

## When a team already exists: customize it

1. Open [Settings > Teams](/app/admin/settings/teams).
2. Check the list, using search and the **Active / Archived** filter if necessary.
3. Select the team you intend to use to open the edit dialog.
4. Update its **name**, **product**, **product vision** and **goal** to reflect your real team.
5. Review **Scrum or Kanban** mode. Changing the mode of an existing team may be restricted; do not create a duplicate to bypass this rule.
6. Save, verify the team details in the list, then select that team in modules that offer a team picker.

## When there is no usable team: create one

1. From [Settings > Teams](/app/admin/settings/teams), choose **Create team** in the page header.
2. Enter the **name** and select **Scrum or Kanban Agile mode**.
3. Complete the **product**, its **vision** and **goal** when these details are available.
4. Create the team and confirm it appears in the list before moving on to members.

## How SMN uses these details

Agile mode determines the available cycle screens: **sprints** for Scrum and **Kanban periods** for Kanban. Team data helps associate and filter business information; product details can be used by the screens and reports that support them.

## Final check

The right team exists with the expected name and mode, product information has been checked, and there are no duplicates. Manually mark **team** done in the checklist only after checking the result.
