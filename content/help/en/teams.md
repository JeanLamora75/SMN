# Your team: start with what is already there

Before tracking your first sprint, take a few minutes to make sure SMN reflects your real team. There is no need to recreate everything: **check existing teams first**.

Depending on the installation or restore process, teams may already be present. A fresh native v33 installation with an empty database does not create a team automatically. Changes here require administrator permissions.

## Already have a team? Make it yours

1. Open [Settings > Teams](/app/admin/settings/teams).
2. Find the team you want. Use search or the **Active / Archived** filter if needed.
3. Select the team to open its edit form.
4. Adapt its **name**, **product**, **vision** and **goal** to your work.
5. Review the **Scrum or Kanban** mode. It determines which cycles are available and may be locked for an existing team.
6. Save and confirm the updated details in the list.

> An existing team is usually a better starting point than a duplicate. If Agile mode cannot be changed, review the situation before creating another team.

## No suitable team yet? Create one

1. In [Settings > Teams](/app/admin/settings/teams), choose **Create team** in the header.
2. Enter a recognizable name and select **Scrum** or **Kanban**.
3. Add the product, vision and goal using the information available today.
4. Confirm, then find the new team in the list.

## Why these details matter

Agile mode determines whether SMN uses **sprints** or **Kanban periods**. The team also provides the context for members, cycles, tracking data and reports.

You can refine details later. For now, the most important thing is having the right team selected.

## Your final check

The team name and Agile mode are correct, product information has been reviewed and there are no duplicates. You are ready to move on to team members.
