# Absence categories: configure SMN with confidence

Categories standardize member absences and improve calendar and capacity interpretation.

> **Camille's tip:** check what already exists, then modify only what is necessary.

## 1. Why is this setting important?

Categories standardize member absences and improve calendar and capacity interpretation. Good configuration makes subsequent data easier to understand and reduces repeated manual work.

## 2. Open the configuration screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Absence categories** in the corresponding section.
3. Use the available entry to open [the settings screen](/app/admin/absence-categories).
4. Check permissions and current records before making changes.

## 3. A step-by-step approach

1. Review existing categories to avoid duplicates.
2. Select Create category when available.
3. Enter the name and the additional fields shown in the form.
4. Save and check the resulting entry.
5. Open a member calendar and verify the category is offered.

## 4. Understand the fields and their impact

Category identifies the absence reason; dates and half-days on the absence record define the affected time.

> **What happens when you save?** The relevant configuration becomes available to its business module. Previous records or generated documents may not be changed retrospectively.

## 5. Advice and troubleshooting

Prefer labels everyone can understand. Categories should support reporting without unnecessarily revealing private details.

If the action is missing, check your role and whether the catalog marks it as unavailable. Review any validation error before repeating the change.

## Your final check

Can you explain what changed, why it matters, and where to verify the result? If yes, the setup is ready for use.
