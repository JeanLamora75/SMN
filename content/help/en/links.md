# Links: find and manage team resources

Keep your team's useful resources close at hand: boards, documents, tools and shared spaces. Links help you find them without hunting through bookmarks.

## Access links

Open **Links** from the navigation when your administrator account is authorized. The screen groups team-specific links and accessible cross-team links.

## Find and open a link

1. Confirm the team name in the header.
2. Select **Cards** or **List** view.
3. Search by link title, then narrow results using **scope** and **category** filters.
4. Sort by title, category or most recent date as needed.
5. Select **Open** on a result to open the destination in a new tab.

## Add or edit a link

With management permission:

1. Select **Add** in the header.
2. Fill in the form, including the title, destination address and available scope or category options.
3. Save and find the new entry in the list.
4. Use the edit action on an existing card or row to change it. Verify its title and destination after saving.

For **deletion**, use the dedicated action and read the confirmation. If a conflict or access error occurs, refresh and review the situation instead of forcing it.

## Categories under Settings

Link categories and related settings are maintained separately in **Settings > Links**. Use that screen to prepare categories before assigning them to links in the main module.

## Why manage links in SMN?

SMN keeps relevant resources easy to find. **Cross-team or team scope** establishes their context, while **categories** support filtering. SMN does not automatically analyze an external website just because its address has been saved.

## Check the result

You can find the right link, open its destination and, when authorized, change its classification. Reading this guide does not modify a link.
