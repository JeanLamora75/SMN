# Ceremony templates: configure SMN with confidence

Templates avoid recreating every Scrum ceremony manually. They provide the rules used when proposing or generating sprint meeting occurrences.

> **Before you change anything:** look at existing settings and consider which teams and users they affect.

## 1. Why configure this section?

Templates avoid recreating every Scrum ceremony manually. They provide the rules used when proposing or generating sprint meeting occurrences. Review it whenever your organization changes, or when a feature's results do not match your expectations.

## 2. Open the right screen

1. Open [Settings](/app/admin/settings).
2. Find **Ceremony templates** in the settings catalog.
3. Select its entry, when available. The related screen is [here](/app/admin/settings/ceremonies).
4. Check your access rights, the selected team and the current values.

## 3. Follow the configuration workflow

1. Open Settings then Ceremonies in a Scrum installation.
2. Review existing templates before selecting Create.
3. Enter the type, name and scheduling fields offered in the form.
4. Check whether the template is active and save.
5. When generating a sprint, review the ceremony preview and then the calendar.

## 4. Understand the fields and their effects

A template rule influences future occurrences; each generated meeting becomes its own business event, editable when permissions allow.

> **What changes after Save?** The resulting configuration is used by the related SMN module. Existing business records or already generated documents are not necessarily changed retroactively.

## 5. Practical guidance

Changing a template does not guarantee retroactive updates to ceremonies already created. Always check existing sprints.

**If the option is unavailable**, check your permissions, the installation mode and whether the Settings catalog marks it as planned or only partially available.

## Your final check

Can you explain **what was changed, why, what it affects and how to verify it in the relevant screen**? If yes, the setup is meaningful and ready for daily use.
